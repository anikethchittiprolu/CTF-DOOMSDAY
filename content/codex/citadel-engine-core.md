Grimoire page, engineering fragment. The Doomsday Engine does not consume matter; it consumes
possibility. Each universe it eats is a universe in which something else could have happened. Doom
designed it to end the Incursions by deleting the choices that cause them. The sums were correct.
Banner checked them twice. Neither of them mentioned that the machine also deleted the choice to
build it.
