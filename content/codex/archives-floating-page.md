Grimoire page, margin note. The floating pages drift between the shelves and cannot be pinned or
bound. A reader who wishes to study one must wait for it to settle, which it does only when the
reader stops trying to read it. Boris says this is the first lesson of the Archives. The second
lesson is that a page which will not be held is often the page most worth reading.
