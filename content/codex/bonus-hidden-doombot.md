Easter egg. A Doombot stands behind the Foundry, facing the wall, humming a lullaby. It has no
orders and no destination. It is, Boris insists, being polite. He has tried speaking to it three
times and each time it turned its head slightly, as if to listen, and then returned to the wall. The
song has no words. Boris has begun to hum along, quietly, and does not intend to stop.
