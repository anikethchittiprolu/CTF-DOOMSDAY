Resistance intercept. We found a bundle of letters in the ash. Most are scorched beyond reading, but
the ink on a few has survived as a faint outline, as though the words had decided to stay. The
letters are addressed to a woman called Cynthia. They begin with an apology and end without a
signature. We have not read the rest. We are leaving them where we found them, under a stone.
