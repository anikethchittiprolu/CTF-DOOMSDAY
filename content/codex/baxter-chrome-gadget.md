Equipment catalogue. The chrome gadget on the middle shelf is labelled harmless. It is not harmless,
but it is not dangerous either; it is merely very, very eager. It solves problems in the lab faster
than the lab can pose them, and it does so without judgement. Reed uses it every day and trusts it
completely, which, Doom would observe, is the only definition of a problem that matters.
