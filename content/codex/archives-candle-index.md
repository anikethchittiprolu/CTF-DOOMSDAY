Diary leaf, an archivist. The index is kept in candlelight because the old stacks reject electric
lamps. Each candle is a catalogue entry; lit, it shows the shelf, and snuffed, it shows nothing. I
learned to read by the flicker. A fast flame means a recent acquisition. A slow flame means
something old and dangerous. A candle that burns blue means somebody is reading the book at this
moment, from somewhere else.
