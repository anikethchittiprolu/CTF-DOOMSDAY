Easter egg. A proof was left in the Engine Core, addressed to nobody. It demonstrates that the
Incursions can be stopped. It also demonstrates that Doom will not like how. The proof is two pages
long and every line is clear. Banner left it on the floor beside the core, folded once, with a
coffee ring in the corner, as though he had been interrupted. He has not been seen at the Engine
since, and Doom has not asked where he went.
