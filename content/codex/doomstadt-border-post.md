Border Post Nine, shift report. Travellers crossing into Latveria are counted, logged, and thanked
for their visit. The guard rotation is posted on the wall of the booth in plain sight, because Doom
holds that a secret everyone can read is no secret at all. Seven travellers last month declined to
leave. The border does not discuss where they went. It records only that they were treated well, and
that their papers were found in order, which in Latveria is the highest praise a document can earn.
