Form 7-C, Office of Public Petitions. Citizens of Doomstadt may submit grievances to the iron box in
the central plaza. All petitions are read. Most petitions are answered with silence, which the
Ministry classifies as a response. One petition in forty is granted. The ministry reports that the
granted petitions share a single feature: the petitioner asked for nothing for themselves. Clerks
are reminded that the box has no lock. Doom considers a lock an insult to those who would never
dare.
