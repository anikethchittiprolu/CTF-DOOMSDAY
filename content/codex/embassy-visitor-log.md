Reception log, New York mission. Visitors to the Embassy sign in, surrender their phones, and are
escorted. The log is kept by hand because paper cannot be remotely edited. Most entries are mundane:
florists, couriers, a man who repairs the clocks. The clock repairman has visited every Tuesday for
eleven years. The clocks have never been repaired. The Embassy has never missed a Tuesday, and
neither, it appears, has anyone who is paying attention.
