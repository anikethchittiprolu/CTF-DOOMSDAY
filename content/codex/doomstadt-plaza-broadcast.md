Broadcast standing order. Every address from the Sovereign is carried by every screen in Doomstadt,
including those in the hospital, the school, and the prison. No citizen is exempt from hearing him.
No citizen has ever been punished for listening poorly. The order notes that Doom speaks briefly,
because a long speech suggests doubt. It also notes, in a smaller hand, that the plaza screens were
installed by a contractor who has since asked to be forgotten.
