Chancery order. The scribes of the Archives copy documents by hand and sign each copy with a cipher
key unique to the scribe. The keys are never written down. A scribe who loses a key is retired with
honours, because no archive can survive a signature it cannot verify. The order notes that the best
forger in Latveria was once a scribe, and that Doom hired him afterwards for exactly that reason.
