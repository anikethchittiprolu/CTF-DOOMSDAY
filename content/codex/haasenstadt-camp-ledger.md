Ledger entry, Haasenstadt camp. The camp keeps a ledger of births, deaths, and debts, in that order,
because the order is the argument. Debts are tallied last and forgiven first. The ledger shows that
in the year of the fire the camp lost four wagons and forty cattle and gained thirty children. The
ledger concludes with a single line: the arithmetic of a community is not the arithmetic of a state.
