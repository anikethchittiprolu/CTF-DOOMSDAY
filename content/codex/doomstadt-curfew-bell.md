Resistance intercept. The curfew bell rings at nine and the city goes dark within a minute. We have
mapped the minute. The grid does not fail at once; it fails district by district, east to west, and
the order never changes. A grid that never changes is a grid that can be read. Someone inside the
control room is leaving the schedule unlocked, deliberately. We do not know whether it is a friend
or a test. We are treating it as both.
