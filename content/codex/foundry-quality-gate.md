Quality control record. Every unit leaving the Foundry passes through the quality gate, where it is
scanned, measured, and asked one question by a senior inspector. The question is not recorded and
the answer is not shared. Units that answer correctly are released. Units that answer incorrectly
are returned to the line and disassembled with care. The inspectors have never explained what the
question is, only that it has been the same since the first unit.
