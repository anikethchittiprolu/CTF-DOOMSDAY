Grimoire page, surveyor's report. The surveyor measured the peak at four thousand metres on Monday
and at six on Tuesday. He reports that both measurements were correct. He was reassigned, and has
since declined all invitations to explain. His final report states that the mountain does not
change; rather, it is the only thing in Latveria that remains honest about not being fixed.
