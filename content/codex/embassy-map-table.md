Diary leaf, an attache. The map table in the study is covered in pins. Red pins are people, blue
pins are money, and the black pins are things nobody will name aloud. I moved a pin by accident last
week and a Doombot arrived within a minute to move it back. It did not speak. It did not need to.
The pin had been on the right place the whole time. I had simply been looking at the wrong map.
