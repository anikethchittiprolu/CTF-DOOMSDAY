Grimoire page, rubbing of an inscription. The inscription inside the mask reads, in the old tongue,
that it will fit only the face that deserves it. Doom put it on while it was still glowing. The
monks have never agreed on whether the inscription was a warning or a prophecy. Strange, who has
read it, only says that it can be read both ways, and that the cruellest reading is usually the
correct one.
