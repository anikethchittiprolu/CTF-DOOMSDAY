Resistance intercept. The diplomatic pouch is inviolable under treaty, so nobody has ever opened
one. We borrowed a pouch for one evening and photographed what we could without breaking the seal.
Inside were folders, a thermos of tea, and a set of blueprints for a machine whose purpose is not
stated. The tea was still warm. Whoever packed it expected the pouch to be opened by someone who
would appreciate it.
