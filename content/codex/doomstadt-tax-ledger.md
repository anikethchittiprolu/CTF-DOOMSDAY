Ledger summary, Treasury of Latveria. Taxes in Doomstadt are low, collected on time, and never
discussed. The treasury employs forty clerks and one auditor, who has not been seen since the ledger
was balanced. Every coin collected funds the walls, the grid, the schools, or the Foundry, in that
order. A footnote in the margin, in a hand not matching any clerk, reads: the sum is correct but the
total is wrong. No clerk has explained the footnote, and none has been asked twice.
