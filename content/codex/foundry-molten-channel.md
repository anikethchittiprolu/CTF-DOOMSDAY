Safety bulletin. The molten channels carry metal at a temperature that makes the factory floor
shimmer. They run beneath grated walkways. Workers are reminded not to look down, not because
looking is dangerous, but because the view is distracting, and a distracted worker is a hazard to
the other thirty on the shift. The channels have never overflowed. The bulletin is reissued every
year regardless, in case anyone has forgotten how to be afraid.
