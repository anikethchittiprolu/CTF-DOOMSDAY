Vehicle log, Baxter Building. The car has flown to the moon, to the sea floor, and once, briefly,
through a wall. Its logbook is kept in pencil, because ink would not survive the journey. The final
entry reads: returned from Latveria, passengers intact, vehicle mildly singed. Under it, in a
different hand, someone has written: he let us go. Nobody in the building has agreed on what that
means.
