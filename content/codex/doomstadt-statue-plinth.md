Municipal record, Statue of the Sovereign. The statue stands thirty metres high and looks east,
toward the border, where the sun rises over the Doombot sentries. Its plinth carries an inscription
that has been replaced three times, each time at Doom's own request, each time with fewer words. The
sculptors were paid in full and were not permitted to sign their work. Children are told the statue
watches over them. The statue, being iron, makes no comment either way.
