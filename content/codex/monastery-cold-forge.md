Grimoire page, forge notes. The forge at the Monastery of the Mask has been cold for centuries, yet
the walls are warm to the touch. The monks do not light it. They believe that the fire is not absent
but waiting, and that to relight it would be to interrupt something. Doom lit it once, as a young
man, and the story goes that it answered him in a voice he has never described.
