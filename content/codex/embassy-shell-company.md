Resistance intercept. The Latverian Embassy in New York owns nothing. Every lease, car, and desk
belongs to a shell company, and every shell company belongs to another. We have followed the chain
through four jurisdictions and one cemetery. The last owner on the paperwork died in nineteen
seventy-one. His signature, however, was renewed last spring. Either a ghost is signing for the
Embassy, or somebody is very good at being one. We suspect the second.
