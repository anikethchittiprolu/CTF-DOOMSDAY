Grimoire fragment, bound in iron. The first Doombot was not built to serve; it was built to be
argued with. Doom wrote its thoughts in plain steel, then told it to disagree. The prime unit has
refused four of the Sovereign's orders and been thanked each time. Its serial number is the only one
in the Foundry that is written as a name. It is the only unit permitted to say no, and it says so
rarely, and only when Doom is wrong.
