Grimoire page, practice notes. The rune table is a slab of black stone covered in worn symbols.
Apprentices trace the runes with a finger and are told to read them aloud. Most read wrongly and
nothing happens. The table was designed to be forgiving. It records only what a reader gets right,
and over the years the sum of those correct readings has slowly become a map of everyone who ever
tried.
