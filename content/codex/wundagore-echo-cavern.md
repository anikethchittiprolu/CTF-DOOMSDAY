Grimoire page, caver's note. The cavern returns an echo, but not your own. A caller hears their
voice answered a moment before they have spoken. Some visitors leave at once. Others stay and ask
questions. The echo, they report, answers truthfully and without comment, and the visitors who stay
longest tend to be those who were never sure they wanted the answer.
