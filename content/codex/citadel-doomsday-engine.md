Grimoire page, the final entry. When the Engine is finished it will be the most powerful machine
ever built, and the only one that will ask its builder a question before it runs. The question has
been written in the old tongue on the housing. It reads: what will you keep? Doom has not answered.
Every previous builder of every previous engine answered at once, and that, the page notes, was the
mistake.
