Grimoire page, Castle Doom. The great cipher wheel turns once an hour and has not stopped in two
hundred years. Scholars have tried to stop it with levers, wedges, and one regrettable cannon. The
wheel holds a message that changes every hour, and the message is always the same message, written
differently. Boris keeps a small notebook of every version. He says the wheel is not hiding
anything. It is only very patient about being understood.
