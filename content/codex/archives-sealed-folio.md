Grimoire page, last in the series. The sealed folio has a cover of black leather and no title. Doom
reads it once a year, alone, on a date he has never disclosed. Boris has seen the date and says it
falls within a week of the anniversary of a death. The folio holds a single page. It is not a spell,
not a plan, and not a confession. It is an apology addressed to no one, written by a man who says he
does not apologise.
