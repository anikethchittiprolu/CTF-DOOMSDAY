Diary leaf, a camp elder. The wagon burned in the third hour of the night. We saved the horses and a
box of letters. By morning the soldiers had come, not to arrest anyone, but to count what was lost,
and to write it down in a book. I asked why. The officer said the Sovereign wished the loss recorded
accurately. I said the loss could not be recorded. He wrote that down too.
