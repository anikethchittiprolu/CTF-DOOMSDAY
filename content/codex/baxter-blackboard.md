Lab notes, Baxter Building, Earth-828. The blackboard in the main laboratory has been erased and
rewritten so often that the slate beneath is a ghost of equations. Reed leaves a single line
untouched at the top: an inequality he has been trying to disprove for eleven years. The line, he
explains, is not an error. It is a promise to himself that he will keep trying, even once he is sure
he will fail.
