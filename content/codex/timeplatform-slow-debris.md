TVA incident report. Debris from the last Incursion hangs motionless above the platform, rotating a
few degrees an hour. The Bureau classifies it as a hazard to navigation. Visitors classify it as the
most beautiful thing they have seen. The report concludes that the debris is a snapshot of a
catastrophe, held mid-breath, and recommends that nobody be allowed to hurry it.
