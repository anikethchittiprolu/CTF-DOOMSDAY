Easter egg. A line of mathematics at the bottom of the blackboard is written in a hand that is not
Reed's. It is, nonetheless, correct. Reed has stared at it for an hour and cannot say who wrote it,
only that the notation is from a century that has not happened yet. He has not erased it. He has
instead written a small question mark beside it, and underlined the question mark twice, which for
Reed is a kind of prayer.
