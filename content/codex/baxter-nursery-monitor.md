Diary leaf, Sue Richards. The nursery monitor in the lab picks up the building's whole hum, the
pumps and the lifts and the late arithmetic. Franklin sleeps through all of it. Reed does not. He
sits beside the monitor at night and listens for a change in the pitch of the hum, and says he can
tell when the universe is about to do something. I tell him it is only the heating. He nods and
keeps listening.
