Grimoire page, field note. A boulder on the lower slope flickers like a faulty screen. Touch it and
for a moment the world around you repeats, a half second of it, once. The rock does not appear to
mean harm. It appears to be stuck, replaying the instant the Incursion touched the mountain. Strange
has sat with it for an afternoon and says it is the loneliest object he has ever met.
