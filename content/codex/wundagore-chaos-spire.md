Grimoire page, final entry. The spire at the summit hums in a key that does not exist. It is where
the Incursion was first felt, and where Strange believes it will first arrive. The spire has no
door, but one can be made by someone who understands what the mountain is made of. The mountain, he
adds, is made of exactly what each climber carries up it.
