Diary leaf, the camp medic. I treated eleven burns last night and one broken heart. The burns will
heal. The broken heart belongs to a boy who watched the wagon fall and asked whether his mother
would have survived had the soldiers come sooner. I told him they could not have come sooner. It was
a lie, and it was the kindest one I have ever told, and I do not regret it.
