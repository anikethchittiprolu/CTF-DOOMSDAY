Resistance intercept. We recovered a firmware image from a decommissioned Doombot. Its motion
controller is excellent and its safety interlocks are strange. The interlock does not prevent harm;
it prevents harm to the wrong people. The distinction is encoded in a table that does not appear
anywhere in the documentation. Whoever wrote it understood the difference between a rule and a
loyalty, and chose to teach the machine only the second.
