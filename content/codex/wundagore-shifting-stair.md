Grimoire page, traveller's note. The stairs of Wundagore do not lead where they led yesterday. A
climber who counts the steps will find a different number each time, and a climber who stops
counting will find the summit sooner. The mountain, locals say, dislikes being measured and holds
the habit against anyone who tries. It prefers to be crossed, humbly, by those prepared to be
surprised.
