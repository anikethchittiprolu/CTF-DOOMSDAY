Easter egg. Beneath the statue plinth, in very small letters, is a single word. The word is not
Latverian. It is the name of a child. Nobody in the plaza reads it, because nobody in the plaza
bends low enough to see. The stonemason who carved it was paid twice, once for the work and once for
his silence, and he has kept both bargains. On the anniversary each year, a single candle appears on
the plinth and is gone by morning.
