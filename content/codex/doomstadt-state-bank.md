Audit note, State Bank of Latveria. The vault door was built to resist a siege and a lawyer, in that
order of difficulty. The bank holds the savings of every household and a reserve that no outside
auditor has ever seen. Interest is paid in full and on time. The audit committee observes that the
bank has never been robbed, and that the only person known to have tried is now its head of
security, a post he holds with visible reluctance and perfect competence.
