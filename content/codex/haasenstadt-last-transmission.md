Resistance intercept. The last transmission from the camp was a thirty-second voice recording. It
contains no instructions, no code, and no names. It is a woman reading a bedtime story to a child,
calmly, as the fire approaches. The recording ends in the middle of a sentence. We have studied it
for three weeks. It is, we believe, the bravest thing anyone in Latveria has ever sent.
