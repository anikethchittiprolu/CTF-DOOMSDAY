Diary leaf, an unnamed visitor. He sat on the throne as though it had been made for someone shorter,
and smiled as though that were the joke. I asked whether he was the master of this place. He said he
was a guest, as everyone is, and that the host had stepped out a long time ago. I believed him. That
is the worst part.
