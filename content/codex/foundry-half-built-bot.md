Diary leaf, a Foundry engineer. There is a Doombot on bench seven with no face. We were told to
leave it as it is. At night its fingers move, very slightly, as if it is counting something. I asked
the shift lead what it was counting and he said it was only a servo settling. I have worked with
servos for twenty years. A servo settles once. This one has settled four hundred times.
