Resistance intercept. The tree at the end of time is dying slowly, one branch every century, and the
branches are universes. At its base the soil is ash. At its crown there is a throne. Those who reach
the throne do not climb the tree; they simply arrive, having stopped asking where it is. We intend
to arrive. Our scouts report the air smells of burning and of rain.
