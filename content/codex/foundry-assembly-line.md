Production note, Doombot Foundry. Line One produces a Doombot every nine minutes and has done so for
six years without a recorded fault. Each unit is tested, serialised, and released into service. The
serial numbers are not sequential; they follow a pattern the engineers describe as elegant and the
night shift describes as unsettling. The line has no supervisor. The line, the foreman wrote in his
last report, supervises itself and would like that noted.
