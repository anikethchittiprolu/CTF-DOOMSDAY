Maintenance manual, section nine. The programmable controller cabinets on the east wall govern the
furnaces, the cranes, and the doors. All cabinets share one panel layout so that a technician from
any shift can read any readout. Technicians are reminded that a green light means the process is
healthy, an amber light means the process is working, and a red light means the process has noticed
you. Do not stand in front of a red light.
