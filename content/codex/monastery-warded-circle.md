Grimoire page, monastery rule. The circle on the forge room floor is drawn in salt and iron filings.
It is to be redrawn at every dawn and never stepped across. Brother Anselm stepped across it once,
to retrieve a dropped lamp, and did not speak again for a year. The rule is old, and it is not a
rule about danger. It is a rule about respect for something that was once alive.
