Easter egg. A ring was found in the ash, too small for any adult hand. It carries an inscription in
a language Doom spoke only to one person. The ring is warm, though the ash around it is cold, and
the camp elders refuse to touch it. They say it does not belong to the camp. They say it belongs to
a woman who is not here, and that it will be collected when she is ready, however long that takes.
