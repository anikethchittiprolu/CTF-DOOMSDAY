TVA procedure. The platform's secondary clock ticks irregularly, by design. Regularity, the Bureau
has found, invites exploitation; any agent who learns the rhythm of a clock will eventually learn to
steal a minute from it. The tick therefore wanders, and the staff measure time not by the clock but
by one another, which most find a good deal harder, and which the Bureau regards as a feature rather
than a flaw.
