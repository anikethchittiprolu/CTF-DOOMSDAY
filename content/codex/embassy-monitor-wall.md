Facilities note, New York mission. The monitor wall in the second floor study displays one hundred
and twelve feeds. The ambassador is permitted to view forty. The rest are reviewed by Doombots that
do not blink, tire, or gossip. The wall was installed after a diplomat was found to be reading his
own mail, which the ambassador described as a lapse in discipline and the Sovereign described as a
promising sign of curiosity.
