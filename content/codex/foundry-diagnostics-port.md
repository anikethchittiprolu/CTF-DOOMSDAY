Service memo. The diagnostics port on each Doombot is located behind the left shoulder plate and is
guarded by a mechanical latch. Technicians connecting to the port are reminded that the unit will
answer honestly, and that its answers are logged. A Doombot cannot lie to a technician, but it can
decline to answer, and the memo notes that declining is itself a kind of statement, one the units
make with unusual eloquence.
