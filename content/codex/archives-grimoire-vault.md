Grimoire page, vault ledger. The vault holds eleven volumes that Doom has forbidden himself to open.
They are not locked. The sovereign, the ledger notes, requires no lock to refuse himself. Three
volumes are about his mother. Four are about the Incursion. The remaining four are blank, and the
ledger lists them anyway, as though the blankness were the content and the archivist had read every
page.
